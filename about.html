About the MCGI Chorale

From eight singers to a global ministry of over 12,000 choristers

The Members Church of God International (MCGI) Chorale, widely known as the Ang Dating Daan (ADD) Chorale, is one of the most prominent religious music ministries in the world. Their history is defined by a journey from a handful of singers to a global organization that set world records.

Our History

1977 – 2000s

Origins and Early History

The chorale serves as the musical arm of MCGI, which was registered in 1977 by Bro. Eliseo "Eli" Soriano.

Small Beginnings: The music ministry originally began with just eight members. In its early years, they supported Bro. Eli's town-to-town preaching and the flagship program, Ang Dating Daan (The Old Path), which launched on radio in 1980.

Growth through Broadcast: As the program moved to television and satellite, the choir's role expanded to leading congregational singing and performing at "Bible Expositions."

2008 – Present

The 24/7 Nonstop Singing

A pivotal moment in their history occurred on August 10, 2008, when Bro. Eli and Bro. Daniel Razon launched the 24/7 Nonstop Singing of Praises.

Biblical Basis: The practice is based on 1 Chronicles 9:33, which describes singers who were "employed in that work day and night."

Global Scale: To maintain unceasing praise, the ministry now involves over 12,000 choristers globally. Members from different time zones (Americas, Africa, Europe, Australia, and Asia) take shifts to ensure the singing never stops — a feat they have maintained for over 17 years.

October 12, 2015

The 2015 Guinness World Record

The MCGI Chorale achieved international recognition during the 35th anniversary of Ang Dating Daan at the Smart-Araneta Coliseum.

The Record: They officially set the Guinness World Record for the "Largest Gospel Choir" with 8,688 participants.

The Performance: The choir performed a 15-minute medley of original praise songs composed by Norman Caraballo Rueda. The medley included four specific songs:

  • "Stand Up"
  • "Praise the Name"
  • "Hallelujah, Amen"
  • "Enrich the Spirit"

Verification: The record was verified using turnstile counters and barcoded tickets, audited by the firm Punongbayan & Araullo.

Leadership & Composition

Key Figures: The chorale is guided by the Overall Servant (Bro. Daniel Razon) and was previously overseen by the late Bro. Eli Soriano. Sis. Ruth Soriano serves as the group's coordinator and adviser.

Structure: The ministry is divided into the Music Ministry Core (the primary performing group) and various Regional/Provincial/City/Locale Choral Groups.

Musical Style & Repertoire

The chorale is known for its synchronized choreography and "solemn yet powerful" vocal arrangements.

Their repertoire consists almost entirely of original compositions specifically written for the church's services and anniversaries, ranging from traditional hymns to contemporary gospel medleys.

History of the MCGI Chorale (Laguna West District)

2001

The KKTK Locale Choir was established in San Cristobal, starting with approximately 15 members.

Feb 2003

All Locale Choirs were promoted to the District level, officially forming the District Choir.

Today

Through God's mercy, the district now has 22 City Level Choirs and 10 Locale Level Choirs.
