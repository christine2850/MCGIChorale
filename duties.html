Choir Duties & Events

Serving God through music in various gatherings and activities

Events

Thanksgiving of God's People

Weekly gathering where the congregation offers praise and thanks to God through songs and worship led by the chorale.

Special Pasalamat ng Buong Bayan

A special thanksgiving event where the entire community comes together to express gratitude through music and worship.

Worship Service

Regular worship gatherings where the chorale leads the congregation in hymns and spiritual songs.

Prayer Meeting

Midweek prayer services where the choir provides musical accompaniment for prayers and devotion.

24/7 Duty

Round-the-clock singing of praises, maintaining the unceasing worship as inspired by 1 Chronicles 9:33.

Harana sa Kapatiran

Musical serenades dedicated to fellow brethren, offering comfort and encouragement through song.

Mass Baptism

Special ceremonies welcoming new members into the faith, accompanied by celebratory hymns from the chorale.

Fiesta ng Dios

Special ceremonies welcoming new members into the faith, accompanied by celebratory hymns from the chorale.

Anniversary Celebration

Musical serenades dedicated to fellow brethren, offering comfort and encouragement through song.

Other Duties & Activities

Mass Indoctrination

Teaching sessions for new believers where the choir provides worship music to enhance the learning experience.

Bible Study

In-depth scripture study sessions complemented by appropriate hymns and spiritual songs.

Mass Blood Letting and Medical Mission

Charitable blood donation drives where choir members participate and provide entertainment through music.

General Assembly

District-wide gatherings of all choir members for updates, fellowship, and collective worship.

Orientation

Introduction sessions for new choir aspirants to understand the duties and doctrines of the ministry.

Practice

Regular rehearsal sessions to perfect vocal techniques, harmonies, and choreography for performances.

Lamay

Wake services providing comfort to bereaved families through solemn hymns and spiritual songs.

Dalaw Orphanage

Outreach visits to orphanages, bringing joy and hope to children through music and fellowship.
